Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026  |  Effective Date: April 14, 2026
This policy applies to CertifyChain and all its associated services.

1. Who We Are

CertifyChain ("we", "us", "our") is a decentralized certificate verification system that uses Ethereum blockchain technology and AI forensic analysis to issue and verify academic and professional certificates.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@certifychain.app

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate the certificate verification service:

  • Certificate Data: Student name, course name, issuing organization, and certificate ID you provide when issuing a certificate.
  • File Hash: A cryptographic SHA-256 hash of any certificate file you upload. The original file is not permanently stored.
  • Blockchain Transaction Data: Transaction hashes and smart contract interaction data recorded on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet (publicly visible by nature of blockchain).
  • IPFS Hash: Content identifier (CID) for files pinned to IPFS.
  • AI Analysis Results: Trust score and forensic analysis output for uploaded documents (stored alongside certificate metadata in our database).
  • Usage Data: Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, endpoint accessed) retained for 30 days.
💡 We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or payment information unless you explicitly provide them for support purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To issue certificates on the Ethereum blockchain
  • To enable third parties to verify certificate authenticity
  • To run AI forensic analysis on uploaded documents
  • To display dashboard statistics to authorized issuers
  • To maintain service security and prevent abuse

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • MongoDB (metadata): Certificate metadata (name, course, org, hashes, AI scores) is stored in our MongoDB database hosted on MongoDB Atlas (cloud, US/EU region).
  • Ethereum Blockchain: File hashes and certificate IDs written to the blockchain are permanent and immutable by design. They cannot be deleted.
  • IPFS: Files pinned to IPFS may persist on the network even after we unpin them.
  • Uploaded Files: Certificate files uploaded for issuance are processed and then deleted from our servers within 24 hours.
  • Server Logs: Automatically deleted after 30 days.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal browser storage:

  • localStorage (cc_consent_v1): Stores your cookie consent preference. No expiry.
  • No third-party tracking cookies are used on this site.
  • No analytics scripts (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are loaded.
You can clear localStorage at any time via your browser's developer tools → Application → Local Storage.

6. Your GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)

If you are a resident of the EU or EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your data from our MongoDB database. Note: data already written to the blockchain cannot be deleted.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18): Request we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@certifychain.app with the subject "GDPR Request".

7. Third-Party Services

  • Ethereum / Sepolia Testnet: Public blockchain — data on-chain is visible to anyone.
  • IPFS / Pinata: Decentralized file storage network. Subject to Pinata's Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Database hosting. Subject to MongoDB's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including HTTPS encryption, private key management via environment variables, file hash verification, and input validation. However, no internet service is 100% secure. Blockchain data, by its nature, is publicly accessible.

9. Children's Privacy

CertifyChain is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of CertifyChain after changes constitutes acceptance.
